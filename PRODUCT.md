# Olan's portfolio

## Platform
Web: a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript portfolio with an interactive Three.js room.

## Purpose
Visitors explore Olan's projects, biography, skills, experience, and contact details through objects in a personal studio. The destination navigation also reaches all five sections without requiring precise 3D interaction.

## Constraints
Preserve the portfolio's factual content, links, camera journeys, audio, and existing alternate-world interaction. Keep desktop, touch, keyboard, and reduced-motion support. Reuse the installed Three.js r128 pipeline and native browser features.

## Approved redesign
The owner requested bright cream and white menus, detailed colorful pixel icons, title icons overlapping panel borders, persistent glow on unexplored clickable objects, removal of the location HUD, an arcade and flower shelves on the left, and a figurine cabinet on the right.

## Arcade
Click the arcade, or choose Play Space Patrol in Help, for a small native Canvas 2D game. Steer left/right with arrows, A/D, touch buttons, or dragging; shooting is automatic. Three lives, escalating enemies, a local best score, pause/resume, and replay. Closing the modal stops the game; leaving the tab pauses it. No additional game engine.
